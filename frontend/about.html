Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

The Everett Hotel was born from a singular dream — to create a sanctuary where every guest feels like royalty. Nestled in the heart of Nairobi City, our hotel blends timeless architectural grandeur with contemporary sophistication.

Every detail has been meticulously crafted to deliver an unparalleled stay. From our hand-selected art collection adorning every corridor to our award-winning culinary programs, we curate an experience that transcends the ordinary. Our philosophy is simple: luxury is not merely a standard — it is a promise.

Purpose & Ambition

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide an extraordinary hospitality experience where every interaction is infused with warmth, elegance, and attention to detail. We strive to exceed expectations by anticipating needs before they arise, creating memories that last a lifetime for every guest who walks through our doors.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the pinnacle of luxury hospitality — a destination where innovation meets tradition, and where every stay becomes a story worth telling. We envision a future where the Everett brand sets the benchmark for sustainable, world-class luxury hotels across the globe.
