Guest Assistance

Find Your Answers

You can make a reservation by visiting our Booking Page, calling our front desk at +254 700 123 456, or emailing us at reservations@everetthotel.com. Online bookings allow you to select your preferred room type, dates, and any special requests. A valid credit card is required to secure your reservation.
Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before your scheduled check-in time. Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in will incur a charge of one night's stay. No-shows will be charged the full reservation amount. To cancel or modify your booking, please contact our front desk or manage your reservation through your account.
Standard check-in time is 3:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in (from 12:00 PM) and late check-out (until 2:00 PM) are available upon request, subject to availability, and may incur an additional fee. For guaranteed early check-in or late check-out, please contact us at least 48 hours in advance.
All rooms include complimentary high-speed WiFi, flat-screen smart TV, air conditioning, in-room safe, ironing facilities, and premium bath amenities. Deluxe rooms and suites additionally feature spa access, mini bar, and city or ocean views. Presidential Suite guests enjoy exclusive butler service, private jacuzzi, and rooftop terrace access.
Yes, Everett Hotel welcomes pets up to 15 kg in designated pet-friendly rooms. A non-refundable pet fee of KSh 7,500 per stay applies. Please notify us at the time of booking if you plan to bring a pet. Service animals are always welcome at no additional charge. Pet amenities including beds, bowls, and treats are available upon request.
Our on-site restaurant "The Everett Kitchen" serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner with a curated menu of local and international cuisine. Breakfast is served daily from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM. Room service is available 24/7 for all guests. Suite guests enjoy complimentary breakfast. We also have a rooftop bar and lounge open until midnight.
We accept M-Pesa, all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), debit cards, and bank transfers. Payment is collected at check-in for the full stay amount. A valid payment method is required at the time of booking to guarantee your reservation. We also accept cash payments at check-out.
Children under 12 stay free when sharing a room with parents. Cribs and rollaway beds are available upon request at no extra charge. Children's menus are available at our restaurant and through room service. Our concierge can recommend family-friendly activities and attractions in the area.
Yes, we offer both self-parking and valet parking. Self-parking is available in our secure parking bay at KSh 2,500 per night. Valet parking is available at KSh 4,500 per night and includes in-and-out privileges. Suite and Presidential guests receive complimentary valet parking. Electric vehicle charging stations are available on the ground floor.
Yes, we provide private airport transfer services from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) and Wilson Airport. Sedan transfers start at KSh 8,500 and SUV transfers at KSh 12,000. Presidential Suite guests enjoy complimentary round-trip airport transfers. Please request transfers at least 24 hours before your arrival or departure through our concierge team.
